Industrial cameras for inspection

We help you choose the right camera type, then supply it with the lens, lighting and software as one working inspection system.

Area-scan cameras

The most common choice. Captures a full image of each part, suited to defect, presence and label checks.

Line-scan cameras

Built for continuous materials such as film, sheet, fabric and long or fast-moving parts.

Smart cameras

Camera and processor in one unit. Simple to install for code reading and basic checks.

3D cameras

Measure height, volume and shape. Used for dimension checks and robot or forklift guidance.

Code readers

Read barcodes, QR codes and direct part marks (DPM) for traceability.

Machine vision questions, answered

What is a machine vision system?

It uses industrial cameras, lighting, lenses and software to inspect products automatically on your line. It checks for defects, missing parts, codes, labels and dimensions, and rejects faulty items in real time.

Which industries in North India use machine vision?

Pharma and packaging in Baddi and Haridwar, automotive and engineering in Ludhiana, Manesar and Pantnagar, FMCG and electronics in Derabassi, Mohali and Noida, plus metal, plastic and food manufacturers across the region.

Can a camera replace manual quality checking?

For repeatable checks such as defects, missing parts, labels, codes and dimensions, yes. A camera inspection system checks every item at line speed and keeps a record. We confirm this on your samples first.

What is the price of a vision inspection system?

It depends on what you inspect, the number of cameras, line speed and the reject mechanism. Send a sample and we will give you a clear quotation after the feasibility test.

Do you supply industrial cameras for production lines?

Yes. We supply industrial cameras with the correct lens, lighting and software, and install them on your line as a complete quality inspection system, including the reject signal to your PLC or machine.

Which machine vision brands do you supply?

We mainly supply Hikrobot, Irayple and OPT as supplier and distributor. We also arrange other brands as per customer requirement.

Do you serve my state?

We are based in Derabassi, Punjab and provide on-site service in Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Jammu. We supply across India. On-site service outside North India depends on order size and quantity.

How do I know if vision can inspect my product?

Send us sample parts or photos with the defects you want to catch. We test them with suitable cameras and lighting and tell you honestly what can be detected before you commit.

Can it connect to my existing line?

Yes. The system is integrated with your line controls, including PLC signals, reject mechanisms and data logging for traceability.
